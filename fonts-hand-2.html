The unique bit

Six things to do with the ampersand

The & you liked was more than a character. Here are ways to make it carry the design.

I. Let the & be the hero
Charlotte & Ciaran
The ampersand set huge between the names, stacked. It becomes the mark itself, and it is the one element nobody else's wedding site has.
II. Two colours, one ampersand
Charlotte & Ciaran
Names in ink, ampersand in a warm terracotta and a touch larger. Subtle, but it makes the middle of the line the focal point.
III. Hide the date inside the loop
Charlotte &3 · 7 · 27 Ciaran
The wedding date set tiny inside the loop of the ampersand. People only spot it when they look properly, which is exactly the kind of detail that gets mentioned.
IV. Knock it off the line
Charlotte & Ciaran
Each part rotated a degree or two, as if written by hand on a table that moved. Reads as genuinely handwritten rather than a font pretending.
V. Reversed out of wine
Charlotte & Ciaran
Cream script on the wine ground, ampersand in a paler peach. Works on the hero and on the footer, and it is the version that will look best on a phone in sunlight.
VI. A mouse in the margin
Charlotte & Ciaran
One of your mice standing next to the names, small, like a signature in the corner of an illustration. Ties the names to the paintings and gives the whole thing a private joke.

The messiest of the ten are 9, 1 and 6. 3 and 7 are the most readable, so they could do headings as well as names. Say a number for the font, and a number for the ampersand idea, and I will build the pair together.
