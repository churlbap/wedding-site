Where to Stay

The venue sits between La Mora and Altafulla on the Costa Daurada. Here are our recommendations — some with special rates for our guests. If you'd like us to hold a room for you, use Register Interest and we'll confirm it directly with the hotel.

May Altafulla Beach Boutique Hotel

May Altafulla is a lovely boutique option right by the beach, with a rooftop bar for drinks with a sea view. We're holding a room block for our guests from €210 per night including breakfast. Resort Beneath the castle

Tamarit Beach Resort & Spa

We stayed here when visiting the venue and loved its little-village feel. We rented bikes and played padel! It's right beneath the castle, so you can stumble home whenever you're ready. They're waiving the usual seven-night minimum for our guests, so shorter stays are welcome. If you're interested, add your details using Register Interest and we'll follow up with the resort.

Hotel Code CHCI27 (CHarlotte & CIaran 27) — 5% off From €200 per night, breakfast included

Hotel Altafulla Mar

If you'd prefer a hotel, Altafulla Mar is a lovely option close to the beach, with pools and restaurants on site. It's also a great base for exploring Altafulla! We loved the town, especially the old town, which is well worth a wander.

Book directly with the hotel and use the code CHCI27 — that's CHarlotte and CIaran, then 27 for the year — at checkout. The code gives 5% off the standard online rate, and breakfast is included. Nothing to register with us — just book straight with the hotel. If the code gives you any trouble, email us and we'll sort it with them.

Hotel Old town

Gran Claustre

If you fancy staying in Altafulla's old town, Gran Claustre is another lovely option. We loved this part of town, it's full of character and lovely for a wander, and has some gorgeous restaurants. Worth a look if you prefer an old-town setting rather than staying right by the beach. Book directly with the hotel — no wedding rate here, but it's a genuinely lovely spot.

Apartments & villas Good for groups

Airbnbs

If you'd rather have your own place, an apartment or villa works really well here — especially if you're coming as a group or staying a few extra days. It's often the best value for a longer trip, and you get a kitchen and a bit of space to yourself.

Where to look: La Mora is the closest to the venue, right by the castle and the beach — handiest for the wedding itself. Altafulla is a couple of minutes further along, with the old town, more restaurants and the station, so it's the better base if you want to be in amongst things.

Our understanding is that most hosts don't open their calendars for our dates until January 2027, so don't be alarmed if there's little available before then — it's well worth checking again after that.
