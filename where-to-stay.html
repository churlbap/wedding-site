Where to Stay

The venue sits between La Mora and Altafulla on the Costa Daurada. Here are our recommendations — some with special rates for our guests. If you'd like us to hold a room for you, use Register Interest and we'll confirm it directly with the hotel.

Resort Beneath the castle

Tamarit Beach Resort & Spa

We stayed here when visiting the venue and loved its little-village feel. We rented bikes and played padel! It's right beneath the castle, so you can stumble home whenever you're ready. They're waiving the usual seven-night minimum for our guests, so shorter stays are welcome. If you're interested, add your details using Register Interest and we'll follow up with the resort.

Hotel Altafulla Mar

If you'd prefer a hotel, Altafulla Mar is a lovely option close to the beach, with pools and restaurants on site. It's also a great base for exploring Altafulla! We loved the town, especially the old town, which is well worth a wander. They're offering a wedding rate for our guests from €200 per night including breakfast — use the code CCHC121 when booking. If the code gives you any trouble, email us and we'll sort it with the hotel directly.

May Altafulla Beach Boutique Hotel

May Altafulla is a lovely boutique option right by the beach, with a rooftop bar for drinks with a sea view. They're offering a wedding rate for our guests from €210 per night including breakfast — use the code CHCI27 when booking online. We're also holding a room block here, so if you'd like a room from the block, add your name using Register Interest and we'll confirm it with the hotel.

Hotel Old town

Gran Claustre

If you fancy staying in Altafulla's old town, Gran Claustre is another lovely option. We loved this part of town, it's full of character and lovely for a wander, and has some gorgeous restaurants. Worth a look if you prefer an old-town setting rather than staying right by the beach. Book directly with the hotel — no wedding rate here, but it's a genuinely lovely spot.
