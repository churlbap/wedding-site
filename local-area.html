Castell de Tamarit sits on the coast between La Mora and Altafulla, about an hour south of Barcelona.

By air

Which airport?

Barcelona El Prat (BCN) is a great choice, around 90 km, roughly 1 hour from the venue by car.

Reus (REU) is only about 20 minutes from the venue, but the flight choice is more limited. Definitely worth a check to see if it works.

By train

Train to Altafulla-Tamarit

There's a small Renfe station right in the village, Altafulla-Tamarit, on the coastal line between Barcelona and Tarragona.

Trains from Barcelona Sants are straightforward and cheap, and the station is right in the town. However, you would have to get to the station from the airport.

Transfers

Private transfers from the airport

Fixed-price private transfers from Barcelona Airport start from around €100 per vehicle.

Operators worth comparing: Titan Transfers, Zone Transfers, Shuttle Direct, MyTransfers and Zip Transfers. Prices vary with time of year and how early you book.

Car hire

Hiring a car

If you are keen to see more than the village, a car is the easiest way. There are hire desks at Barcelona Airport and Reus, and the drive down is straightforward.

It also frees you to explore: Tarragona's old town and Roman amphitheatre, the walls of Montblanc, the monasteries at Poblet and Santes Creus, and the beaches at La Mora and Creixell.

By bus

Buses along the coast

There are local bus services along the coast linking Altafulla with Torredembarra, La Mora and Tarragona, and longer-distance coaches run from Barcelona and Tarragona down the coast. They're cheap, and useful if you're staying nearby and don't want a car.

Local taxis

Getting around once you're here

Altafulla and La Mora are small, and there's no Uber or Bolt. Local radio-taxi firms cover Altafulla, Torredembarra and the surrounding villages.

We will be running shuttles to and from the local town, so this is why we are recommending staying close.
