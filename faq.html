How do I get there?

Castell de Tamarit sits on the coast between La Mora and Altafulla, about an hour south of Barcelona and 20 minutes north of Tarragona.

From Barcelona El Prat (BCN) is the easiest airport, with the most flights from the UK and Ireland.

• By road: about 90 km, roughly 1 hour 5 minutes on the AP-7. Toll road, straightforward driving.

• By train: take the airport train or the Aerobús into Barcelona Sants, then a Renfe regional train down the coast to Altafulla-Tamarit. Trains run regularly and it's cheap. From the station it's a short taxi ride to the hotels or the castle.

• Private transfer: fixed-price transfers start from around €100 per vehicle with meet & greet, a car taking up to 4 and minivans 5 to 8. Worth comparing Titan Transfers, Zone Transfers, Shuttle Direct, MyTransfers and Zip Transfers. If a few of you land around the same time, share a minivan, as it works out far cheaper per person.

From Reus (REU) is only about 20 minutes from the venue, but far fewer flights and often seasonal. Brilliant if something convenient lines up; not worth a convoluted connection.

• By road: roughly 25 km, about 25 minutes by taxi or hire car.

• By train: Reus has a station on the same coastal line, so you can connect down to Altafulla-Tamarit, but it's usually quicker and easier to take a taxi.

From Girona (GRO) is about 1 hour 50 minutes by road. Only worth it if the flight price is dramatically better.

Once you're in the town: Altafulla and La Mora are small and walkable, but there's no Uber or Bolt. Local radio-taxi firms cover Altafulla, Torredembarra and the surrounding villages. Book in advance, especially for the wedding night, as the fleet is small and it's peak season. We'll share the numbers we're using closer to the date.
